Beta Testing Information

What invitation-only testers should know.

Thank you for testing Northstar. Your feedback helps us identify confusing experiences, content issues, visual problems, and technical defects before public release.

Eligibility

The invitation-only beta is intended for adults age 18 and older. Participation is optional and you may stop testing at any time.

What to expect

  • Features may change, fail, or be temporarily unavailable.
  • Some information may be staging, demo, cached, estimated, or unavailable.
  • You may encounter visual defects, incomplete cloud behavior, or performance issues.
  • Use your own judgment about activity safety, venue rules, weather, equipment, consent, and suitability.

Feedback

Use the in-app feedback form when online. For support or when the form is unavailable, email northstarapp.support@gmail.com.

Accounts and data

Core Northstar features can be used without creating an account. Account-enabled features may provide backup, synchronization, multi-device continuity, recovery, or other cloud services.

No payment expectation

Unless you are specifically told that a store sandbox purchase test is occurring, the invitation-only beta is not intended to charge you. If you see an unexpected purchase screen or charge, stop and contact support.

Policies

By participating, review the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Data Deletion Policy.
